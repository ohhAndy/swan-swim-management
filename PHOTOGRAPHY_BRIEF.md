# 📸 Photography Creative Brief & Shot List
**Client:** Swan Swim School (Markham & Newmarket, ON)  
**Project:** Website Redesign & Brand Campaign  
**Target Audience:** Parents of young children (ages 6mo–12yrs)  
**Core Brand Message:** Water confidence, gentle coaching, warm 90°F water, and smiling kids.

---

## 1. Creative Direction

* **The Goal:** Capture authentic, candid moments of joy, courage, and connection between children and their instructors.
* **The Look & Feel:** Warm, sunlit, vibrant, and approachable.
* **What to Avoid:** Posed stiffness, shivering/distressed kids, dark/muddy pool reflections, or sterile facility shots with no people.
* **Key Angle Tip:** Water-level perspective (eye-level with the children) creates the most engaging, intimate connection.
